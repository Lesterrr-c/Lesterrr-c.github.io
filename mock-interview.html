01 Tell me about yourself. +
SAMPLE ANSWER

Good day. My name is Lester Lagajino, and I am a fourth-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student at Datamex College of Saint Adeline. I am developing my skills in web development, databases, and technical troubleshooting.

I have been working on practical projects, including a spa appointment management system using PHP and MySQL. I also have hands-on experience assisting with technical troubleshooting for LED wall systems. I want to continue learning and apply my knowledge to real IT challenges.

Practice tip: Aim for 45–60 seconds. Focus on your education, relevant experience, and career direction.

02 Why did you choose BSIT? +
SAMPLE ANSWER

I chose BSIT because I am interested in how technology works and how it can solve practical problems. I wanted to learn how applications are developed, how databases manage information, and how computer systems can be maintained and improved.

As I progressed through my studies, I became more interested in building applications and troubleshooting technical problems. I want to continue developing these skills into a professional career.

03 What are your strongest IT skills? +
SAMPLE ANSWER

My strengths are my willingness to learn, systematic troubleshooting, and practical web development. I have worked with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for my portfolio, and I am developing a PHP and MySQL appointment management system.

I also try to investigate problems carefully instead of immediately assuming their cause. When something does not work, I examine the possible causes, test changes, and review the result.

Be ready to demonstrate: Show a real feature from your portfolio or spa system and explain how it works.

04 What is your weakest skill? +
SAMPLE ANSWER

One area I am still improving is debugging more complex code, especially when several components depend on one another. Sometimes a problem in one file can affect another part of the application.

I am addressing this by making smaller changes, reading error messages, testing each change, and learning to understand existing code before modifying it. This helps me become more systematic and independent when solving problems.

Choose a genuine weakness that you are actively improving. Avoid claiming this answer if it does not reflect your experience.

05 Tell us about your major project. +
SAMPLE ANSWER

One of my main development projects is JNIEL's Body Massage and Spa appointment management system. It is a web-based application that I am developing using PHP, MySQL, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

The system includes customer-facing functions and administrative workflows for managing services and appointments. During development, I have worked through issues involving booking behavior and application logic.

The project has helped me understand how frontend interfaces, server-side code, and database operations work together. It has also taught me the importance of testing the complete workflow rather than checking only whether a page loads.

Be prepared to explain the actual features you completed, the technologies you used, and any remaining limitations.

06 What was your biggest technical problem? +
SAMPLE ANSWER

One challenging technical problem I encountered during hands-on work was persistent flickering on an LED wall. The issue required investigating several possible causes instead of immediately replacing one component.

The potential causes included the processor, power supply, network cable, configuration settings, and receiving card. It was challenging because testing one component did not automatically establish the definitive cause.

The root cause and successful repair were not confirmed, so I would describe the case as an investigation rather than a completed repair.

07 How did you solve it? +
SAMPLE ANSWER

I approached the problem systematically. We ran the processor's built-in test, tried another processor, replaced the power supply and RJ45 cable, and checked the LED configuration settings.

After those checks, the receiving card was identified as a suspected source of the flickering. However, the final root cause and repair outcome were not verified.

The experience taught me to isolate possible causes, document what has been tested, and avoid declaring a solution successful without verification.

This is a useful troubleshooting example because it demonstrates your method without overstating the result.

08 How do you handle deadlines? +
SAMPLE ANSWER

I handle deadlines by identifying the required deliverables, breaking larger tasks into smaller steps, and prioritizing the most important work first. I also allow time for testing and revisions instead of leaving everything until the last minute.

If I encounter a blocker that may affect the deadline, I investigate it early and communicate the issue rather than waiting until the deadline has passed.

09 How do you work with a team? +
SAMPLE ANSWER

I believe good teamwork requires clear communication, shared responsibilities, and respect for other people's ideas. I try to understand my assigned tasks, provide progress updates, and ask for clarification when requirements are unclear.

I am also willing to receive feedback and help investigate problems. When working on a group project, I believe the team should focus on completing the work together rather than blaming individuals when difficulties arise.

Add a real group-project example and describe your specific contribution.

I understand that I am still growing professionally, so I am open to feedback and continuous improvement. I want to contribute to the team while building the skills needed to become a reliable IT professional.

11 What technology are you currently learning? +
SAMPLE ANSWER

I am continuing to improve my skills in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript while developing my understanding of PHP and MySQL through my web application project. I am also learning Git and GitHub for version control and project publishing.

In addition, I am studying cybersecurity fundamentals, including SQL injection prevention and secure coding practices. I try to connect what I learn with practical examples so I can explain not only what a technology does, but also when and why it is useful.

I want to grow into a capable IT professional with strong technical and problem-solving skills. My current interests include web application development, IT support, and system troubleshooting.

In the near term, I want to gain professional experience, improve my programming and database skills, and learn from experienced colleagues. As I gain more experience, I hope to identify a specialization where I can contribute effectively and continue developing my expertise.
